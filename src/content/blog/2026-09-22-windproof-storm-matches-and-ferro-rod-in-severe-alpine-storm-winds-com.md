Windproof storm matches are essential for igniting fires under challenging conditions. They provide a safe and effective method to start a fire, even in the face of strong winds and harsh weather.

### Ferro Rods
Ferro rods are the core component in many windproof matches. They provide a consistent and reliable source of ignition, which is crucial for survival situations.

#### Technical Breakdown

- **Material**: Iron or steel with a pyrophoric coating.
- **Ignition Mechanism**: When scraped against the ferro rod, it produces sparks that ignite the match head.
- **Durability**: High resistance to wear and tear, ensuring long-lasting use.
