## Windproof Storm Matches

### Engineering Specifications
- **Ignition Temperature:** 250°C (482°F)
- **Flame Duration:** 1.5 seconds
- **Water Resistance:** 300 mm hydrostatic head
- **Wind Resistance:** Can function in winds up to 60 km/h (37 mph)

### Material Breakdown
- **Ferric Chloride Core:** The core is treated with ferric chloride, which reacts vigorously upon contact with oxygen. This reaction produces an intense flame.
- **Plastic Encasement:** The exterior encasing is made from a high-density polyethylene material that provides both water resistance and windproof properties.

### Field Analysis
In the boreal forest, low light conditions and potential rain or snow necessitate reliable ignition sources. Windproof storm matches are ideal for starting fires in these environments due to their robust design. They can ignite even when wet and function effectively in windy conditions, ensuring that campers have a consistent method of creating warmth and cooking fires.

## Ferro Rod
### Engineering Specifications
- **Ignition Temperature:** 2000°C (3632°F)
- **Flame Duration:** 4 seconds
- **Wind Resistance:** Can function in winds up to 150 km/h (93 mph)
- **Water Resistance:** 700 mm hydrostatic head

### Material Breakdown
- **Metallic Ferroceramic Core:** The core is made from a ferroceramic material that contains metal particles. When struck, the metal particles heat up and ignite the pyrophoric compound.
- **Plastic Encasement:** Similar to storm matches, the encasing is designed to resist water and wind.

### Field Analysis
Ferro rods are an essential tool for any outdoor expedition in the boreal forest due to their extreme durability and reliability. They can be used repeatedly without wear or damage, making them a long-term investment. Their high ignition temperature ensures that they remain effective even when conditions become severe. Additionally, their ability to function in strong winds makes them invaluable during stormy weather.
