## Field Maintenance Drying Techniques

### 1. **Emergency Shelter and Tent Repair**

#### **Materials Needed:**
- **Tent Repair Kit:** Includes thread (60 Denier polyester), needle, waterproofing spray (hydrostatic head of 15,000 mm), and repair patches (60/200 GSM laminated film).
- **Sunscreen and Sunscreen Wipes**: SPF 30+ for preventing skin damage from reflected sunlight.

#### **Procedure:**
1. **Identify the Damage:** Inspect the tent fabric for any tears, holes, or loose seams.
2. **Prepare the Area:** Clean the damaged area with a damp cloth to remove dirt and debris.
3. **Apply Waterproofing Spray:** Use the spray liberally over the entire surface of the tear before patching to ensure it adheres properly.
4. **Cut Out a Patch:** Measure and cut a square or rectangle from the repair patch larger than the tear by about 1 cm on each side.
5. **Thread the Needle:** Thread the needle with polyester thread and knot both ends securely.
6. **Stitch the Patch In Place:** Begin stitching around the perimeter of the patch, using a backstitch technique to ensure strength and neatness.
7. **Allow Time for Drying:** Let the area dry completely before use.

### 2. **Boot Repair**

#### **Materials Needed:**
- **Waterproof Boot Gaiter:** Made from nylon with a hydrostatic head of 10,000 mm to protect against water intrusion.
- **Adhesive:** High-strength waterproof adhesive (minimum peel strength of 4N/cm).
- **Cotton Padding and Waterproof Liners:** To replace or add for comfort.

#### **Procedure:**
1. **Remove the Gaiter:** Carefully remove any mud or debris from the gaiter’s exterior.
2. **Apply Adhesive:** Apply a thin, even layer of adhesive to the damaged area on both the inner and outer sides.
3. **Position the Patch:** Place the new patch over the tear and press firmly with gloved hands to ensure good adhesion.
4. **Allow Drying:** Allow the gaiter to dry completely before reattaching it.

### 3. **Hardshell Jacket Maintenance**

#### **Materials Needed:**
- **Waterproof Hardshell Jacket:** Made from Gore-Tex fabric (20,000 mm hydrostatic head) for maximum breathability.
- **Silicone Spray:** For repelling water and dirt without compromising breathability.

#### **Procedure:**
1. **Inspect the Jacket:** Check for any signs of wear or damage, such as loose seams or tears.
2. **Clean the Surface:** Wash the jacket with a mild soap and warm water to remove dirt and stains. Air dry thoroughly before applying any treatments.
3. **Apply Silicone Spray:** Use a spray nozzle to apply a thin layer of silicone over the entire surface of the jacket. Ensure it covers both the exterior and interior linings.
4. **Allow Drying Time:** Allow at least 24 hours for the fabric to fully dry and reapply if necessary.

### 4. **Sleeping Bag Repair**

#### **Materials Needed:**
- **Down Sleeping Bag with 800+ Fill Power Insulation:** Ensures warmth and weight efficiency.
- **Waterproof Liner:** For added protection against moisture.
- **Needle and Thread:** To repair any tears or loose stitching.

#### **Procedure:**
1. **Identify the Damage:** Check for any visible damage to the shell fabric or insulation.
2. **Clean the Area:** Gently clean the damaged area with a damp cloth to remove any dirt.
3. **Patch the Shell Fabric:** Use waterproofing spray on the exterior and then stitch over the patch using needle and thread to secure it in place.
4. **Replace Insulation if Necessary:** If insulation is compromised, replace sections of the bag with new down clusters.

## Emergency Supplies Maintenance

### 1. **Tent Stakes and Guy Lines**

#### **Materials Needed:**
- **Sturdy Replacement Stake Pins:** Made from stainless steel for durability and strength.
- **Fabric Repair Kit:** For any minor fabric tears or wear on guy lines.

#### **Procedure:**
1. **Check for Wear:** Inspect all stakes and guy lines for signs of wear, such as fraying or splitting.
2. **Replace Worn Items:** Replace any worn-out stakes with new ones to ensure they hold firm in various terrain conditions.
3. **Repair Guy Lines:** Use the fabric repair kit to patch any tears or weak spots on guy lines.

### 2. **First Aid Kit Maintenance**

#### **Materials Needed:**
- **Waterproof First Aid Kit Case:** Made from durable nylon (140 Denier) with a minimum R-value of 3.5 for thermal insulation.
- **Plasters and Bandages:** Ensure they are sealed in their original packaging to remain sterile.

#### **Procedure:**
1. **Check Expiry Dates:** Verify that all items within the kit have not exceeded their shelf life.
2. **Replace Expired Items:** Replace any expired bandages, plasters, or other consumables with fresh stock.
3. **Repack the Kit:** Place all items back into the waterproof case, ensuring they are organized and accessible.

### 3. **Emergency Blankets**

#### **Materials Needed:**
- **Mylar Emergency Blanket:** With a minimum fill power of 400 to ensure effective insulation against cold temperatures.
- **Waterproof Covering:** To protect from moisture and wind.

#### **Procedure:**
1. **Inspect the Blanket:** Check for any tears or punctures in the Mylar layer.
2. **Repair Tears:** Use waterproof tape (minimum width of 5 cm) to cover any damage, ensuring it adheres securely.
3. **Store Properly:** Keep the blanket in a dry and cool place when not in use.

### 4. **Headlamp Maintenance**

#### **Materials Needed:**
- **Rechargeable Headlamp:** With adjustable brightness settings and a long-lasting battery life (minimum 12 hours).
- **Replacement Batteries:** For continued operation.
- **Microfiber Cloth:** To clean the lens and exterior of the headlamp.

#### **Procedure:**
1. **Check Battery Life:** Test the battery to ensure it has sufficient charge.
2. **Replace Batteries if Necessary:** Insert new batteries or recharge as needed.
3. **Clean the Lens:** Use a microfiber cloth to wipe down the lens, ensuring clear visibility during night use.

### 5. **Water Purification**

#### **Materials Needed:**
- **Portable Water Filter:** With a filter size of 0.2 microns for effective removal of bacteria and protozoa.
- **Chemical Treatment Tablets:** For backup purification in case of filter failure.

#### **Procedure:**
1. **Test the Filter:** Ensure it is functioning correctly by running water through the system to check for any blockages or leaks.
2. **Replace Filters if Necessary:** Change the filter element according to the manufacturer’s guidelines.
3. **Store Tablets Safely:** Keep chemical treatment tablets in a dry, airtight container.
