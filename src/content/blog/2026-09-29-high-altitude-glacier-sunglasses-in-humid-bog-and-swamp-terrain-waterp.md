## Hydrostatic Head Test: Ensuring Water Resistance

The hydrostatic head test measures a material's resistance to water penetration, crucial for sunglasses that must withstand humidity and moisture from fog, rain, and dew. The hydrostatic head rating is expressed in millimeters (mm), with higher numbers indicating better waterproof performance. For high-altitude glacier sunglasses intended for humid bog and swamp terrain, a minimum hydrostatic head of 15,000 mm is recommended to ensure that the lenses stay clear and fog-free during extended use.

### Technical Breakdown: Hydrostatic Head

- **Hydrostatic Head Test**: Measures the height (in mm) of water required to cause water penetration through a fabric sample.
- **Recommended Rating**: 15,000+ mm H2O
- **Material Considerations**: Polyurethane-coated nylon or polyester with reinforced seams for added durability.
