## Field Drying Procedures

### Immediate Post-use Drying

1. **Immediate Evacuation from Tent**: Upon returning from a hike, remove the mat immediately and place it in a dry location away from direct sunlight.
2. **Air Circulation**: Allow for air to circulate around the mat by placing it on a well-ventilated surface or propping it up with sticks if necessary.
3. **Avoid High Temperatures**: Do not use heaters, stoves, or direct sunlight as they can cause uneven drying and potentially damage the mat.

## Tear Repair Guide

### Identifying and Assessing the Damage

1. **Visual Inspection**: Regularly inspect your mat for any signs of wear or damage.
2. **Pressure Test**: Apply gentle pressure in areas that appear damaged to determine if they are weak spots.


> **Field Rig Pick:** For harsh field exposure, verified [Headlamp]([https://www.amazon.com/s?k=](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=)headlamp+outdoor+gear&tag=basecamprig-21) provides reliable durability and safety margins.

### Tools Required

- Replacement foam strips (ensure they match the thickness and density of your current mat).
- Utility knife or sharp scissors.
- Clear tape or high-strength adhesive (such as 3M VHB tape).

### Step-by-step Repair Process

1. **Prepare the Area**: Clean the area around the tear with a dry cloth to remove any dirt or debris that could compromise adhesion.
2. **Mark the Cut Lines**: Use your utility knife or scissors to carefully cut along the edges of the tear, ensuring you do not damage surrounding areas.
3. **Apply Foam Strips**:
   - Align the foam strips so they cover the tear and extend slightly beyond the edges.
   - Press firmly into place to ensure good contact with the mat's surface.
4. **Secure the Repair**:
   - Use clear tape or high-strength adhesive to secure the foam strips in place.
   - Apply a thin layer of adhesive around the perimeter, ensuring it adheres well to both the old and new material.

### Post-Repair Care

1. **Avoid Heavy Loads**: Do not use the mat for several days after repair to allow the adhesive to fully cure.
2. **Check Regularly**: Periodically inspect the repaired area to ensure it remains secure and does not develop further damage.

## Additional Tips for Extended Use

- **Waterproofing Sprays**: Consider using a waterproofing spray designed for closed-cell foam mats (e.g., Nikwax or 3M Scotchgard) to enhance water repellency.
- **Layer Protection**: Place the mat between two thin layers of fabric, such as a groundsheet and a thin tarp, to protect it from abrasions and punctures.
