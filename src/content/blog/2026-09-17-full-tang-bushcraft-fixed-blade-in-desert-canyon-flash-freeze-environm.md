## Technical Breakdown

### Blade Material and Design
- **Steel Type**: 1095 High Carbon Steel (HCS)
  - **Hardness**: 58-60 HRC
  - **Properties**: Ideal for bushcrafting due to its balance of hardness, edge retention, and ability to take a good grind.
- **Blade Design**: Full-tang design ensures stability and strength during heavy use. The blade is designed with a slightly recurved tip for both practical and aesthetic reasons.

### Handle Material
- **Handle Type**: Micarta with G10 Reinforcement
  - **Properties**:
    - **Micarta**: A composite material made from paper impregnated with epoxy, providing excellent grip.
    - **G10 Reinforcement**: Adds extra strength to the handle while maintaining light weight.

### Grips and Safety Features
- **Non-Slip Grip**: The handle includes a textured surface for superior traction in all conditions.
- **Full Tang**: Ensures that the blade is securely attached, providing stability during heavy use.

## Environmental Considerations

### Protection from Extreme Temperatures
- **Flash-freeze Environments**: The knife must withstand rapid temperature changes without affecting its functionality. 
  - **Material Durability**: Both steel and handle materials are chosen for their ability to maintain integrity under extreme conditions.
  
### Protection from Sand and Dust
- **Sealing Mechanism**: The knife’s blade tip is sealed using a high-quality epoxy that prevents dust and sand ingress.
