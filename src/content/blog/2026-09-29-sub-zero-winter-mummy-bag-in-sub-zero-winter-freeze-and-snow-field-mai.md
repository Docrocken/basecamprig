### Field Maintenance: Drying Techniques
Proper drying of a sub-zero mummy bag is crucial to maintain its insulating properties and overall functionality.

#### Natural Air Drying
- **Procedure**: Lay the sleeping bag flat in a warm, dry area away from direct sunlight or heat sources. Allow it to air out for several hours.
- **Advantages**: Environmentally friendly and cost-effective.
- **Disadvantages**: Slow process and may require more space.

#### Using a Tent and Heat Source
- **Procedure**: Place the sleeping bag inside a tent with a portable camp stove or a hot water bottle. Ensure good airflow to prevent condensation buildup.
- **Advantages**: Faster drying time compared to natural air drying.
- **Disadvantages**: Requires additional gear such as a tent and possibly a heat source.

#### Using a Tarp
- **Procedure**: Lay the sleeping bag on top of a tarp in a sheltered area. Use the tarp to collect any moisture and allow it to dry under sunlight or wind.
- **Advantages**: Protects from external elements while drying.
- **Disadvantages**: Limited space efficiency.

### Field Maintenance: Tear Repair
Tears can occur due to rough handling, sharp objects, or exposure to harsh weather conditions. Immediate action is necessary to avoid further damage and loss of insulation.

#### Cleaning the Area
- **Procedure**: Clean the torn area with a mild soap solution and a soft-bristled brush to remove any dirt or debris.
- **Tools Needed**: Mild dish soap, soft-bristled toothbrush, clean cloth.

#### Applying Patches
- **Material**: Use a high-quality waterproof patch kit designed for outdoor gear. Ensure it includes a suitable adhesive compatible with the sleeping bag's fabric.
- **Procedure**:
  1. Clean and dry the torn area thoroughly.
  2. Apply a thin layer of the provided adhesive to both sides of the tear.
  3. Place the patch over the tear, ensuring a tight seal around the edges.
  4. Smooth out any air bubbles with your fingers or a credit card.
  5. Allow it to dry completely before use.

#### Reinforcing Edges
- **Procedure**: Apply additional adhesive along the edges of the patch for added strength and durability.
- **Tools Needed**: Small brush for application, scissors for trimming.
