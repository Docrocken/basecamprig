# Packable Camp Hatchet on Fast-and-Light Packrafting Expeditions: Long-term Durability Analysis in Harsh Conditions

## Introduction
Fast-and-light packrafting has gained popularity among outdoor enthusiasts for its efficiency and adaptability. One of the essential tools for such expeditions is a packable camp hatchet, which serves multiple purposes including setting up camps, cutting materials, and emergency repairs. This guide delves into the long-term durability analysis of packable camp hatchets in harsh conditions specific to fast-and-light packrafting.

## Material Specifications
### Blade Material: 1095 High Carbon Steel
- **Hardness:** Rockwell C48-52
- **Corrosion Resistance:** Mildly resistant; requires regular oiling or waxing

### Handle Composition
- **Handle Core:** Birch Wood
- **Coating:** Polyurethane
- **Thickness:** 1/4 inch (6.35 mm)

## Design Considerations for Harsh Conditions
### Impact on Blade and Handle
In fast-and-light packrafting, the hatchet must withstand impacts from rocks and branches while being light enough to carry. The blade is crafted from high carbon steel, which provides sufficient hardness but remains lightweight. Birch wood handles are chosen for their durability and natural water resistance.

## Field Analysis: Durability in Harsh Conditions
### Testing Environment
- **Temperature Range:** -20°C to 45°C (-4°F to 113°F)
- **Humidity Levels:** 10% to 90%
- **Prolonged Exposure:** Simulated by storing hatchets in various environments for extended periods

### Test Conditions
- **Drop Testing:** From a height of 2 meters onto concrete, replicated over 50 cycles.
- **Impact Resistance:** Subjected to repeated impact from a 1 kg weight at varying angles and speeds.
- **Moisture Exposure:** Placed in conditions with relative humidity above 90% for 7 days.

### Results
The high carbon steel blade demonstrated excellent resistance to cracking, even after multiple drops. The polyurethane coating on the handle remained intact, preventing moisture penetration and maintaining grip despite prolonged exposure to wet conditions.

## Maintenance Tips
- **Regular Cleaning:** After each use, clean the blade with a soft cloth to remove dirt and moisture.
- **Oiling:** Apply thin layers of oil on the blade and handle to prevent rust.
- **Storage:** Store in a dry place away from direct sunlight to avoid warping the handle.
