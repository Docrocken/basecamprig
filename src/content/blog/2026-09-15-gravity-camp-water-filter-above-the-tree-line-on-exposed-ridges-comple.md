# Gravity Camp Water Filter Above the Tree Line on Exposed Ridges: Complete Field Selection and Material Breakdown

## Introduction
Above the tree line on exposed ridges, gravity-fed water filters are essential for ensuring a reliable and safe supply of drinking water. This field guide delves into the technical considerations required to select appropriate materials and components for such an environment.

## Key Components of Gravity Filter Setup
### Water Source Selection
Choosing a reliable water source is critical. Typically, high-altitude streams or snowmelt runoff provide clean enough water sources that require minimal filtration. However, it's essential to carry a chemical treatment kit as a backup (e.g., Aquamira or Katadyn Micropur) due to the risk of cryptosporidium and giardia.

### Filter Body
The heart of any gravity-fed filter is its core. For high-altitude environments, filters with a capacity between 1500-2000 mL are ideal. The Sawyer Squeeze is a popular choice, featuring:

- **Material**: PTFE (Polytetrafluoroethylene) membrane for 0.1 micron filtration.
- **Capacity**: 1870 mL.
- **Flow Rate**: Up to 95 drops per second at 6 psi.
- **Water Capacity**: 2000 mL.

### Water Storage and Collection
A reliable water storage container is crucial, especially in unpredictable weather conditions. A large, durable hydration bladder (e.g., Platypus 4.3L) or a high-quality backpack with integrated reservoirs (e.g., Osprey Atmos AG 65) are recommended.

- **Material**: Nalgene HDPE (High-Density Polyethylene) for durability and UV resistance.
- **Volume**: 4.3 liters for the Platypus, 40+ liters in a [backpack]([https://www.amazon.com/s?k=](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=)[backpack](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=[backpack](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=backpack+outdoor+gear&tag=basecamprig-21)+outdoor+gear&tag=basecamprig-21)+outdoor+gear&tag=basecamprig-21) reservoir setup.

### Pump or Bag
A gravity-fed system uses either a pump or a bag to push water through the filter. The Sawyer Squeeze uses a simple squeeze mechanism:

- **Pump Material**: Silicone.
- **Bag Capacity**: 1870 mL.

## Tent Selection and Setup
Selecting an appropriate tent is essential for shelter during setup, which might take several minutes due to the rigidity of packing materials in cold conditions. A 2-person tent with a fly (e.g., Big Agnes Flyック) provides adequate protection from wind and rain.

- **Fabric**: Ripstop nylon.
- **Waterproof Rating**: 10,000 mm hydrostatic head.
- **Fill Power**: Not applicable; focus on weight-to-space ratio and durability instead.

## Personal Gear
### Footwear
Sturdy boots with good traction are essential for navigating uneven terrain. GORE-TEX waterproof membranes ensure feet stay dry while hiking up steep ridges.

- **Material**: GORE-TEX.
- **Waterproof Rating**: 28,000 mm hydrostatic head.
- **Insulation**: Thinsulate lining for warmth in cold conditions.

### Clothing
A hardshell jacket (e.g., The North Face Stormy Weather) is crucial for wind and rain protection. Fleece layers provide additional insulation when needed.

- **Material**: Ripstop nylon with DWR (Durable Water Repellent) finish.
- **Insulation**: 200 g/m² fill power, 120 Denier face fabric.
- **Breathability**: 5,000 mm/24hr water column.

### Sleeping Bag
For overnight stays, a high-quality sleeping bag is essential. A temperature-rated bag with a down filling provides warmth in cold conditions.

- **Temperature Rating**: -30°C (-22°F).
- **Fill Power**: 650 fill power.
- **Material**: Insulated baffles to prevent cold spots.

### Backpack
A lightweight, high-capacity backpack (e.g., Osprey Atmos AG) is necessary for carrying all gear. Ensuring the pack has a well-padded back panel and comfortable hip belt is crucial for long hikes.

- **Capacity**: 65 liters.
- **Material**: Stretch ripstop nylon with DWR finish.
- **Weight Distribution**: Designed to distribute weight evenly across the hips and shoulders.

### Camp Stove
A portable camp stove (e.g., MSR Pocket Rocket) is useful for boiling water or cooking meals. Ensuring it has a wind-resistant design is crucial in exposed ridges.

- **Fuel Type**: White gas.
- **Weight**: 180 grams.
- **Heat Output**: 2,500 BTU/hr at full power.

### Headlamp
A high-quality headlamp (e.g., Black Diamond Spot) with a rechargeable battery is essential for navigating in the dark or during setup.

- **Bulb Type**: LED.
- **Lumens**: 1300 lumens.
- **Battery Life**: Up to 6 hours on high brightness mode.
