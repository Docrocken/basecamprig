### Field Maintenance: Drying Techniques

#### Immediate Post-Use Cleaning
After use in a wet environment:
1. **Rinse with Water:** Use clean, fresh water to rinse off any visible debris or sap.
2. **Dry Thoroughly:** Use a soft cloth or towel to dry the blade and handle. Ensure all areas are dry before storage.

#### Drying Methods for Moisture Protection
- **Air-Dry Indoors:** Place the saw in an indoor area with good air circulation, ensuring no direct heat sources.
- **Use Dryer:** For faster drying, place the saw on a clean, flat surface and use a low-dry setting dryer. Avoid high heat as it may damage the blade material.

### Repair Guide: Tear Repairs

#### Identifying Tears
Common areas for tearing include:
1. **Handle Straps:** Due to frequent contact with trees.
2. **Folding Mechanism Hinges:** Friction and moisture can cause wear over time.

#### Tools Required
- Small Scissors or Utility Knife
- Threaded Needle (size 8-10)
- Matching Thread (polyester, color-matched for aesthetics)
- Sewing Machine (optional)

#### Repair Procedure

##### Step-by-Step Guide:
1. **Prepare the Area:**
   - Clean the torn area thoroughly to ensure a strong bond.
   - Use alcohol or another cleaning solution to remove any dirt.

2. **Stitching with Needle and Thread:**
   - For smaller tears, use a needle and thread to sew along the edges of the tear. Start from one end and work towards the other.
   - Ensure each stitch is tight and secure to prevent further tearing.
