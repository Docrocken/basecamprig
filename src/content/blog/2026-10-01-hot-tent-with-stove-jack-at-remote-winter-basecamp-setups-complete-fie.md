2. **Stove Jack**
3. **Camp Stove**
4. **Sleeping Bag**

## Stove Jack Installation

### Importance of Proper Ventilation
Proper ventilation is vital for safety when using a stove inside the tent. The stove jack must allow for sufficient air exchange to prevent carbon monoxide build-up.

### Design and Specifications
The stove jack should be designed with multiple inlets and outlets, ensuring adequate airflow without compromising the internal temperature. A minimum of 200 square inches (1300 cm²) of opening area is recommended.
