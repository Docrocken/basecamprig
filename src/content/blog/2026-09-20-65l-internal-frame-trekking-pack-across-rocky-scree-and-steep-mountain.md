### Hydrostatic Head
The pack is designed to withstand moisture, featuring a **3000mm** hydrostatic head, which ensures that water will not penetrate the pack under normal conditions.

### Base Layer
Use lightweight, moisture-wicking base layers to keep the body dry and comfortable during physical exertion. A **200g merino wool top** paired with **150g synthetic bottoms** ensures both warmth and breathability.

### Mid-Layer
For added warmth in colder climates, use a **60g Primaloft mid-layer jacket**. This lightweight insulation offers excellent thermal retention while maintaining packable size and weight.

### Outer Shell
The outer shell should consist of:
- **Outer Jacket**: A **500D nylon ripstop with 10,000mm hydrostatic head** for durability and water resistance.
- **Pants**: **420D nylon twill with 8,000mm hydrostatic head**, providing adequate protection against abrasions.

### Hydration System
A **2L hydration bladder** ensures a constant supply of water. Place it in the main compartment to keep it easily accessible and close to your body for quick sips during breaks.

### Tool Kit
Include essential tools such as:
- A **multi-tool with knife, pliers, screwdrivers**, and other necessary components.
- **Headlamp batteries** stored separately from the main pack to prevent damage from moisture or impact.

### Navigation Tools
Carry a **portable GPS unit** along with a **topographic map** of the area. Ensure these are well-padded within your daypack for protection during transit.
