## Hydrostatic Head Test: Measuring Water Resistance

The hydrostatic head test measures how much pressure a fabric can withstand before water starts to penetrate, quantifying its waterproofness. A higher hydrostatic head rating indicates better resistance against water penetration. For outdoor footwear, a minimum of 10,000 mm H2O is recommended for most winter conditions.
