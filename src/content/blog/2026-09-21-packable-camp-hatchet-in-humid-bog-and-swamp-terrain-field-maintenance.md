### Field Maintenance: Drying Techniques

#### Initial Moisture Removal
After use in humid conditions, the first step is to remove any surface moisture. Use a clean towel or cloth to wipe off excess water from both the blade and handle.

1. **Air Dry:** Place the hatchet in a dry, well-ventilated area out of direct sunlight.
2. **Cloth Drying:** If immediate drying is necessary, use a soft, absorbent cloth to gently pat down any remaining moisture on the blade and handle.
3. **Avoid Direct Sunlight:** Direct sunlight can cause rapid temperature changes that may warp or damage the GRN handle.

#### Extended Drying
For more thorough drying, especially in overnight scenarios:

1. **Use a Dehumidifier Bag:** Place the hatchet inside a dehumidifying silica gel bag to absorb any residual moisture.
2. **Storage Container:** Store the hatchet in a watertight container with desiccant packets for long-term storage.

### Tear Repair: Step-by-Step Guide

#### Identification of Damage
Inspect the blade and handle regularly for signs of damage, such as cracks or tears. Common issues include:

- **Blade Dulling:** Caused by repeated use on hard materials.
- **Handle Cracking:** Due to impact or excessive moisture exposure.

#### Repair Materials
For minor repairs, you will need:

- **Wood Glue (Polyurethane-based)**
- **Sandpaper (200-400 grit)**
- **Wax or Oil**

#### Blade Repair

1. **Inspect the Blade:**
   - Identify any chips or cracks on the blade.
   
2. **Remove Rust and Debris:**
   - Use a wire brush to remove rust, dirt, and debris from the affected area.

3. **Apply Wood Glue:**
   - Apply a thin layer of polyurethane-based wood glue directly onto the damaged area.
   
4. **Sand Down:**
   - Allow the glue to dry completely (24-48 hours).
   - Sand down the repaired area with 200-grit sandpaper until smooth.

5. **Apply Wax or Oil:**
   - Apply a thin layer of wax or oil to seal and protect the repair.
   - Buff it out with a soft cloth.

#### Handle Repair

1. **Identify Cracks:**
   - Look for any cracks in the GRN handle, especially around impact points.

2. **Prepare the Crack:**
   - Use sandpaper (200-400 grit) to clean and roughen the edges of the crack.
   
3. **Apply Wood Glue:**
   - Apply a thin layer of polyurethane-based wood glue directly into the crack.
   
4. **Clamp or Tape:**
   - Place a small clamp or use tape across the crack to hold it in place while the glue dries (24-48 hours).
   
5. **Sand and Finish:**
   - Once dry, sand down any excess glue with 320-grit sandpaper.
   - Apply a thin layer of wax or oil for protection.

### Preventive Maintenance

To minimize the need for repairs:

1. **Use Proper Technique:** Always use the hatchet in a controlled manner to avoid damaging the blade or handle.
2. **Regular Cleaning:** Clean the hatchet after each use with a damp cloth and dry thoroughly before storage.
3. **Protective Covers:** Store the hatchet in a protective sheath when not in use.
