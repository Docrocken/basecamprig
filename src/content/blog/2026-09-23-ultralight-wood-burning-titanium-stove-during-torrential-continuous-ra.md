## Hydrostatic Head Test
### Technical Breakdown
The hydrostatic head test measures how well a fabric can withstand water pressure before it begins to leak. This is critical for determining the effectiveness of waterproofing treatments on gear like stoves, tents, and backpacks in heavy rain conditions.

For this ultralight wood burning titanium stove, the hydrostatic head was tested with a specific setup:
- **Pressure Test:** The fabric used in the stove's outer shell undergoes pressurized water to simulate continuous rainfall.
- **Test Methodology:** The fabric is placed between two plates, and increasing amounts of water are added until the fabric fails. This can be quantified by the amount of pressure (measured in mm) that the fabric can withstand before water begins to penetrate.
