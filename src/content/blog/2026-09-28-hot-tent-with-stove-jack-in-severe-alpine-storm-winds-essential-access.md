# Hot Tent with Stove Jack in Severe Alpine Storm Winds: Essential Accessories and Modular Rig Configuration

## Introduction to the Challenge
Surviving a severe alpine storm while maintaining heat in your tent requires meticulous planning and the right equipment. The combination of extreme winds, low temperatures, and heavy precipitation demands specialized gear that can withstand these conditions. This guide focuses on the setup of an insulated tent with a stove jack, essential accessories, and modular rig configuration to ensure comfort and safety.

## Tent Selection

## Stove Jack Installation
### Purpose:
A stove jack allows the use of a propane or white gas stove inside the tent, providing warmth and cooking facilities. It must be installed correctly to prevent carbon monoxide poisoning and ensure proper ventilation.

### Installation Steps:
1. **Positioning:** Mount the stove jack on a secure surface outside the tent entrance.
2. **Ventilation:** Ensure the stove jack has adequate airflow by placing it in a well-ventilated area.
3. **Safety Measures:**
   - Install a carbon monoxide detector.
   - Use a low-burning, well-sealed stove to minimize smoke and fumes.

## Modular Rig Configuration
### Key Components:
1. **Tent Basecamp:** Set up the tent on a solid ground to prevent it from being blown away by strong winds.
2. **Insulation Layer:** Use a thermal blanket or reflective insulation under the tent for additional warmth.
3. **Ventilation System:** Install vents and ensure proper airflow around the stove jack.
4. **Sleeping Arrangement:** Position sleeping bags on an insulated foam pad to maintain body heat.
