### Field Maintenance Protocols

Regular maintenance is crucial to ensure the longevity and performance of your foldable solar panels. Here are some key steps:

#### Cleaning
- Use a soft cloth or sponge with mild soap and water to clean the surface. Avoid using abrasive materials that can scratch the outer layer.
- For stubborn dirt, use a mixture of warm water and baking soda, but avoid applying pressure that could damage the cells.

#### Storage
- Store panels in a dry place when not in use. Use desiccants like silica gel packets to absorb moisture.
- Keep them flat or folded neatly without creasing. Over time, constant folding can reduce their efficiency.

### Drying Procedures

Drying is essential if your solar panel gets wet, as water can degrade the performance of the cells and backsheet. Here are recommended steps:

#### Quick-Dry Method
1. **Remove Moisture**: Gently shake off excess moisture.
2. **Air Dry Indoors**: Place in a dry, well-ventilated area away from direct heat sources like radiators or heaters. Ensure it dries naturally without being exposed to extreme temperatures.

#### Extended Drying Process
1. **Use a Hairdryer on Cool Settings**: Carefully blow air onto the panel to accelerate drying.
2. **Avoid Direct Heat Sources**: Prolonged exposure to high temperatures can damage the cells and backsheet.
3. **Monitor for Condensation**: Check regularly; if condensation forms, allow it to dry before proceeding.

### Tear Repair Guide

Tears or punctures in foldable solar panels can significantly impact performance. Here are steps to repair minor tears:

#### Tools Required
- Non-abrasive adhesive tape (such as 3M solar panel repair tape)
- Utility [knife](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=knife+outdoor+gear&tag=basecamprig-21) for precise cuts
- Clean cloth

#### Steps to Repair
1. **Prepare the Area**: Carefully clean around the tear with a utility knife or scissors, ensuring no debris remains.
2. **Apply Adhesive Tape**: Cut a piece of adhesive tape slightly larger than the tear and apply it over the damaged area. Ensure it adheres well by pressing firmly along the edges.
3. **Check for Air Bubbles**: Gently smooth out any air bubbles under the tape using your fingers or a dull tool.
