### Technical Breakdown

A hollow fiber membrane filter pump utilizes micro-filtration technology, where water is forced through microscopic pores in a semipermeable membrane. The size of these pores typically ranges from 0.1 to 1 micron, allowing the passage of water while trapping contaminants such as protozoa, bacteria, and larger particles.

### Abrasion Test

Abrasion testing evaluates how well a material resists wear and tear from frictional forces. In outdoor settings, this is critical for the longevity of waterproof components and overall performance during continuous rain.

#### Methodology

1. **Sample Preparation**: The filter pump's cover and housing are subjected to controlled abrasion.
2. **Abrasion Device**: A device that simulates real-world wear by applying abrasive action with a rotating brush or wheel.
3. **Observation Time**: The sample is tested for durability over 50,000 cycles.

#### Results

The filter pump's cover and housing should withstand at least 50,000 cycles without significant degradation in waterproof properties. This ensures that the device remains effective even after extended use in harsh conditions.
