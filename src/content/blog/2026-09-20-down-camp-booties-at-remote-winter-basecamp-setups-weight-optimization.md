## Design Requirements

### Purpose
Down camp booties are designed primarily for insulation in cold weather environments where heat loss is a significant concern. They need to provide warmth, moisture management, durability, and fit comfortably over regular winter hiking boots or crampons.

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
1. **Insulation**: R-values must be high enough to withstand temperatures down to -40°C.
2. **Water Resistance**: Hydrostatic head of at least 15,000 mm to prevent water penetration.
3. **Breathability**: Must allow for moisture vapor transfer without sacrificing insulation.
4. **Durability and Comfort**: Must hold up over long periods with regular use while providing comfort.

## Material Specifications

### Outer Shell
- **Fabric Type**: GORE-TEX® Pro 20,000 mm/20,000 g/m² (breathable and waterproof)
- **Denier**: 1680 D nylon ripstop for durability
- **Color**: Dark colors to minimize UV exposure

### Insulation
- **Fill Material**: Ultra-lightweight down with a fill power of at least 750+ down feathers per square inch (DUPSI) to ensure warmth without weight.
- **Weight**: Maximum 140 grams per pair for weight optimization, critical in backpacking scenarios.

### Lining
- **Material**: Polyester fleece or synthetic insulation material that maintains its insulating properties even when wet

## Practical Application and Usage

### Layering Strategy
Booties are part of a layered approach to cold-weather camping. They should be used in conjunction with thermal socks, base layers, and extremity protection gear like mittens or gloves.

### Integration with Other Gear
Down camp booties should integrate seamlessly with other winter gear:
- **Tents**: Ensure they fit under the tent for additional warmth.
- **Boots**: Designed to work well over regular winter hiking boots or crampons.
- **Sleeping Bags and Pads**: Provide insulation when used in conjunction with a sleeping bag rated for -40°C.

### Maintenance and Care
Proper care is essential to maintain the performance of down camp booties:
- Rinse with cold water after use.
- Air dry in a well-ventilated area away from direct heat sources.
- Avoid washing in hot water or using fabric softeners.
