## Technical Breakdown

### Solar Panel Charger Design

The core component is the [solar panel](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=solar+panel+outdoor+gear&tag=basecamprig-21) charger, which must be both efficient and rugged enough to handle challenging conditions. Key features include:

- **Power Output**: 10W (5W x2 panels)
- **Foldable Design**: Ensures compact storage while providing ample surface area for sunlight exposure
- **Input Voltage Range**: 6V - 34V DC, compatible with various power sources including car batteries and portable generators

### Waterproof Hydrostatic Head Test

The waterproofing of the solar panel charger is crucial to ensure it can operate in wet conditions. The hydrostatic head test measures how much water pressure a fabric or material can withstand before becoming permeable.

- **Hydrostatic Head**: 10,000 mm
- **Specs Explanation**: This means the material can resist up to 10 meters of water column without leaking. For high-altitude conditions where rain and snow are common, this level of waterproofing is essential for maintaining operational integrity.

### Abrasion Resistance

The exterior fabric must be highly durable to withstand constant exposure to windblown sand, abrasive particles, and frequent handling during setup and pack transport.

- **Fabric Type**: 500 Denier Cordura®
- **Specs Explanation**: This material provides a balance between durability and flexibility. The 500 Denier rating indicates it can handle moderate abrasion without tearing or wearing through quickly.

### Other Technical Specifications

- **Weight**: 240 grams (including panels, cables, and connectors)
- **Battery Capacity**: 16,800 mAh Li-ion battery
- **Temperature Range**: -4°F to 95°F (-20°C to 35°C)

## Setup and Operation

In exposed ridges, wind can significantly impact the efficiency of solar charging. The foldable design allows for quick setup to maximize exposure:

- **Setup Time**: 3 minutes
- **Optimal Angle**: Adjust panels manually to face directly towards the sun, accounting for local latitude and time of day.

Above the tree line, temperatures can drop dramatically. Ensuring the solar panel charger remains operational in cold conditions is critical:

- **Thermal Insulation**: Minimal thermal insulation required due to lightweight design
- **Insulation Layer**: 200 g/m² down fill with a R-value of 4.5
