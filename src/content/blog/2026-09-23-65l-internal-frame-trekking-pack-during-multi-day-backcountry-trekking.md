## Design and Construction

### Internal Frame vs. External Frame

The internal frame pack offers a streamlined design that minimizes bulkiness, making it ideal for long-distance treks where every gram counts. Unlike external frames, which can add weight and increase the overall profile of the backpack, an internal frame design allows for better load distribution across the back panel, reducing strain on the shoulders and improving stability.

## Ventilation and Comfort

### Mesh Panels

Multiple mesh panels are strategically placed throughout the pack, enhancing airflow and reducing moisture build-up inside the backpack. Proper ventilation also helps regulate body temperature, keeping the wearer cool without overcooling.

### Contoured Backpanel

The contoured backpanel conforms to the natural shape of the spine, providing a comfortable fit that reduces pressure points and ensures even weight distribution. This design is crucial for long treks where constant carrying can cause discomfort if not properly managed.

## Maintenance and Care

Proper maintenance of the 65L pack extends its lifespan and maintains performance. Regular cleaning with mild soap and water or a specialized backpack cleaner helps remove dirt, grime, and odors that could otherwise compromise comfort and hygiene during extended use. Storing the pack in a dry place when not in use prevents damage from moisture and pests.

### Cleaning Instructions

1. **Remove All Gear**: Carefully unpack all contents of the pack.
2. **Gentle Washing**: Use a soft-bristled brush or cloth to clean any visible dirt or stains.
3. **Spot Clean Only**: For stubborn marks, apply a small amount of backpack cleaner directly to the affected areas.
4. **Air Dry**: Allow the pack to air dry completely before packing away.
