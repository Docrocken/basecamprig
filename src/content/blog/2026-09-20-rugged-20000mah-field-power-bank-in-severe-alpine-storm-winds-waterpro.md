### Design Specifications

#### Waterproofing
The Rugged 20000mah power bank is engineered to withstand severe weather conditions, particularly heavy rainfall. The waterproof rating of the device meets a hydrostatic head test of **1500 mmHg**, indicating its ability to repel water effectively. This rating ensures that moisture will not penetrate the enclosure even in torrential downpours.

#### Abrasion Resistance
The exterior of the power bank is made from **600D nylon fabric** with a **420D polyurethane coating**. The 600D nylon layer provides durability and strength, while the 420D polyurethane coating enhances its abrasion resistance, making it suitable for rugged terrain where rocks and branches might cause wear.

### Thermal Insulation

#### Temperature Regulation
The power bank's thermal management system is designed to maintain optimal operating temperatures. It incorporates a **thermoelectric cooler (TEC)** that can operate within the range of -20°C to 60°C, ensuring reliable performance in freezing cold and hot conditions.

### Breathability and Ventilation

#### Micro-Perforated Panels
The case features **micro-perforated panels** strategically placed around the battery compartment. These tiny holes allow for airflow without compromising waterproofing. This design helps dissipate heat efficiently, preventing overheating during charging or use in warm climates.
