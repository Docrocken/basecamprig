## Technical Specifications

### R-Value
The key metric for any sleeping bag is its thermal performance, measured by the R-value. For a 3-season use in dense boreal forests, an R-value of 5.0 is ideal, ensuring warmth without bulkiness. This R-value translates to maintaining a comfortable body temperature down to around -10°C (14°F).

### Hydrostatic Head
Hydrostatic head measures the bag's resistance to water penetration. A hydrostatic head of 600 mm ensures that light rain or snow will not compromise its insulating properties, essential for navigating through dense forests with unpredictable weather.

### Fill Power and Down Quality
Down quality is measured by fill power, which indicates how fluffy and insulating the down is. An 800-fill down offers excellent warmth-to-weight ratio, crucial in a 3-season context where weight and bulk are significant factors. Higher fill power means better insulation with less material.

### Fabric Material
The outer shell fabric should be both durable and water-resistant. A 400 Denier nylon or polyester is sufficient for 3-season use but ensures longevity and water resistance, essential in a dense forest environment.

## Material Breakdown

### Outer Shell
- **Material**: 400 Denier nylon or polyester with a DWR (Durable Water Repellent) finish.
- **Purpose**: Protects the inner insulation from moisture while providing sufficient durability for outdoor use. A DWR treatment helps to repel light rain and snow, keeping the down dry.

### Insulation
- **Material**: 800-fill down with a minimum of 95% white goose down.
- **Purpose**: Provides excellent thermal insulation without adding excessive weight or bulk. White goose down offers better loft retention compared to grey goose down due to its lower oil content, making it more resilient in wet conditions.

### Inner Lining
- **Material**: Polyester microfleece or synthetic insulation as an alternative.
- **Purpose**: Enhances warmth retention and comfort by providing additional insulation against the skin. Microfleece is preferred for its lightweight and soft feel.
