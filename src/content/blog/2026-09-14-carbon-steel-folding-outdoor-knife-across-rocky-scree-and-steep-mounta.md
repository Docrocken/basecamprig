### Essential Accessories

#### Knife Sheath
- **Material:** Water-repellent nylon with a durable coating.
- **Fit:** Designed to fit snugly around the knife, providing a secure hold and preventing accidental deployment.
- **Features:** Quick-release mechanism for easy access in emergencies, reinforced stitching at stress points.

#### Carabiners
- **Type:** D-shaped carabiners made from 50mm steel with a minimum breaking strength of 22kN (2200kg).
- **Color Coding:** Different colored carabiners can be used to distinguish between primary and secondary tools, enhancing organization and safety.

#### Webbing Loops
- **Material:** High-denier nylon, at least 18mm in diameter.
- **Design:** Reinforced double stitching every 30cm to prevent wear from frequent use.

### Field Protocols

When using a carbon steel folding [outdoor knife](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=outdoor+knife+outdoor+gear&tag=basecamprig-21) on rocky terrain, it is essential to follow specific protocols to ensure safety and efficiency:

#### Edge Maintenance
Regularly sharpen the blade using a portable ceramic honing rod or diamond file. This can be done in less than 30 seconds and ensures the knife remains functional throughout your trip.

#### Handle Protection
Apply a thin layer of beeswax or silicone-based lubricant to the handle periodically to prevent slippage, especially when dealing with wet or icy conditions.

#### Task-Specific Use
- **Cutting Rope:** Use the blade in a controlled manner to avoid unnecessary force that could dull the edge.
- **Prying Rocks:** Employ the knife's back edge for prying rather than the sharp tip to reduce risk of injury and maintain edge integrity.
- **Creating Shelters:** Use the knife’s strength and precision to cut branches or create small notches in tree trunks, ensuring stability and safety.
