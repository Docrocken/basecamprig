## Operational Considerations

### Safety Measures
- **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)**: Always wear gloves, goggles, and sturdy boots when operating the saw in wet conditions to prevent cuts or slips.
- **Proper Storage**: Store the saw with a protective sheath to maintain its integrity and functionality over time.

### Maintenance and Cleaning

- **Inspection**: Regularly inspect the blade and handle for any signs of wear or damage, especially in wet environments.
