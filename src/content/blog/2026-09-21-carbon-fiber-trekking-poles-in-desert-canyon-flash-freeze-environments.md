## Maintenance and Care

### Cleaning
Regularly clean poles using a soft cloth or brush to remove dirt and grime. Avoid soaking in water, as this can lead to permanent damage.

### Storage
Store trekking poles in a dry place when not in use. Use protective covers if storing them for extended periods.
