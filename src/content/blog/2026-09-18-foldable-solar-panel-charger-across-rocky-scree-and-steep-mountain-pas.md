# Foldable Solar Panel Charger Across Rocky Scree and Steep Mountain Passes: Complete Field Selection and Material Breakdown

## Introduction
For outdoor enthusiasts traversing rocky scree and steep mountain passes, a reliable foldable solar panel charger is essential for maintaining electrical devices. This guide provides an in-depth analysis of the materials and specifications required to ensure optimal performance under challenging conditions.

## Key Considerations for Foldable Solar Panels
1. **Durability**: High-impact environments require robust construction.
2. **Weight and Portability**: Minimalist design for ease of carrying over long distances.
3. **Power Output**: Adequate wattage to charge devices quickly.
4. **Weather Resistance**: Protection from elements, including dust and minor water splashes.

## Material Breakdown
### Frame and Shell
- **Frame Material**: Aluminum alloy (6061-T6) for lightness and strength. The alloy offers a balance between weight and durability, ideal for backpacking conditions.
- **Shell Material**: Durable polyester fabric with a polyester coating to enhance water resistance.

### Solar Panels
- **Cell Type**: Monocrystalline silicon cells are chosen for their higher efficiency rates (around 20%) compared to polycrystalline cells.
- **Panel Size and Configuration**: Multiple smaller panels can be combined to increase overall power output while maintaining portability. Each panel measures approximately 15 inches by 30 inches.

### Connector and Wiring
- **Wiring**: High-grade silicone-coated copper wiring for durability and flexibility, capable of withstanding repeated folding.
- **Connectors**: Quick-release connectors made from rugged nylon to ensure easy attachment and detachment without causing damage.

## Field Performance Analysis

### Durability
The use of 6061-T6 aluminum alloy ensures that the solar panel can withstand impacts during trips through rocky scree. The polyester fabric, coated with a polyurethane layer, resists minor water splashes and dust accumulation.

### Weight and Portability
Each individual solar panel weighs approximately 3 pounds (1.4 kg), making them manageable to carry over long distances. Multiple panels can be folded into a compact size for storage in backpacks or gear pouches.

### Power Output
- **Peak Power**: Each 0.96 square meter panel provides up to 20W of peak power.
- **Charge Time**: Under full sunlight, it takes about 4 hours to fully charge a typical smartphone from 0% to 100%.

### Weather Resistance
The polyester coating on the shell material offers a hydrostatic head rating of 5000 mm, making the panels resistant to light rain and snow. The fabric is also treated with a breathable layer that allows moisture to escape while keeping water out.
