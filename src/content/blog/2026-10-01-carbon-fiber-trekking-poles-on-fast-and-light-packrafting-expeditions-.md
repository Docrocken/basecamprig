## Modular Rig Configuration

A modular rig configuration allows for flexibility and adaptability, optimizing the pole's use based on specific expedition needs. This involves selecting appropriate poles, adjusting them according to terrain conditions, and integrating them with other gear items.

### Selecting Carbon Fiber Trekking Poles

When choosing carbon fiber trekking poles for fast-and-light packrafting:

- **Length**: Opt for adjustable poles that can be shortened or lengthened as needed.
- **Materials**: Ensure the poles are made from high-quality carbon fiber to withstand harsh conditions.
- **Tip Design**: Look for sturdy, non-slip tips that can grip various surfaces.

### Adjusting Poles on the Fly

Adjustability is key in a modular rig. Poles should be easy to extend or retract quickly and securely. This allows you to change their length based on whether you are walking upriver or downriver, or even using them as a support while fishing.

## Integrating Trekking Poles into Your Rig

Integrating carbon fiber trekking poles effectively requires careful consideration of how they will be used throughout the expedition:

1. **River Navigation**: Use poles to steady yourself on slippery rocks or unstable surfaces.
2. **Hiking**: Employ them for additional support during long hikes, especially when carrying a heavy packraft.
3. **Camping**: Utilize them as a backup in case of tent setup difficulties.

### Practical Examples

For example, if you encounter a particularly treacherous section of river with large boulders and swift currents, adjust your trekking poles to their full length for maximum stability. Conversely, when setting up camp or carrying the packraft on land, shorten them to reduce bulk and improve handling.
