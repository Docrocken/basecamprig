## Introduction to the Gear

### Technical Overview
When venturing into humid bog and swamp terrain, a high-performance hard shell jacket is essential for maintaining body heat, providing protection from moisture, and ensuring comfort. A 3-layer waterproof breathable hard shell jacket combines functionality with durability in challenging outdoor conditions.

### Key Features
- **3-Layer Construction**: Two outer layers of fabric bonded to an inner lining.
- **Waterproofing**: Hydrostatic head rating of 20,000 mm ensures exceptional protection against rain and moisture.
- **Breathability**: 15,000 g/m²/24h guarantees efficient moisture transfer.
- **Wind Resistance**: High wind resistance due to the outer layer material.

## Temperature Ratings and Comfort Limits

### Cold Weather Performance
The 3-layer hard shell jacket offers significant warmth retention due to its high wind resistance and moisture management properties. The jacket is designed to handle temperatures down to -10°C (14°F) with minimal insulation, making it suitable for cold conditions in humid environments.

#### Temperature Limits:
- **Lower Limit**: -10°C (-14°F): At this temperature, the jacket can maintain body heat effectively.
- **Upper Limit**: 25°C (77°F): The jacket provides adequate breathability to prevent overheating, ensuring a comfortable experience within this range.

### Humidity and Wetness Management
In humid bogs and swamps, managing moisture is crucial for maintaining comfort. The GORE-TEX® Pro fabric ensures that sweat and body heat are efficiently transferred out of the jacket, preventing condensation build-up inside the garment.

#### Moisture Management:
- **Breathability**: 15,000 g/m²/24h: This breathability rating allows for significant moisture transfer.
- **Waterproofing**: A hydrostatic head of 20,000 mm prevents water from penetrating the jacket fabric.
