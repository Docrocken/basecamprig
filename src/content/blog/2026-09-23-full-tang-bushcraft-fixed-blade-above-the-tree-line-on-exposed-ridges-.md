# Full-tang Bushcraft Fixed Blade Above the Tree Line on Exposed Ridges: Temperature Ratings and True Comfort Limits

## Introduction
Operating above the tree line in exposed ridges presents unique challenges for outdoor enthusiasts. The combination of harsh winds, thin air, and variable temperatures requires robust gear to ensure safety and comfort. This guide focuses specifically on the use of a full-tang bushcraft fixed blade knife as part of your kit, with emphasis on temperature ratings and true comfort limits.

## Full-tang Bushcraft Fixed Blade Knife
A full-tang bushcraft fixed blade is an essential tool for navigating challenging terrains. The term "full tang" refers to the blade extending through the entire handle, providing better balance, strength, and durability. When selecting a knife designed for use above the tree line on exposed ridges, several engineering specifications are critical:

### Blade Material
- **Steel Type:** High-carbon stainless steel (e.g., 1095 or D2), known for its edge retention and corrosion resistance.
- **Hardness Rating:** Typically between Rockwell C58-63, ensuring the blade can withstand repeated use in abrasive conditions.

### Handle Construction
- **Handle Material:** Micarta, G10, or other composite materials provide excellent grip even when wet. These materials are also lightweight and durable.
- **Tang Type:** Full tang with a spine that runs the length of the handle for additional structural integrity.
