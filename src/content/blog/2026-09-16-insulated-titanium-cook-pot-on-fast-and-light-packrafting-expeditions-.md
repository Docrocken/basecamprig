## Design Considerations

### Durability and Impact Resistance
A well-designed insulated titanium cook pot should be impact-resistant to withstand drops or knocks during use. This is particularly important in fast-and-light packrafting where equipment might experience more vibrations and impacts compared to land-based activities.

### Ventilation Holes
Ventilation holes are critical for preventing condensation buildup, which can lead to water ingress into the insulation layer. These should be strategically placed around the pot’s circumference without compromising its thermal efficiency.

## Safety Features

### Impact of Insulation on Heat Transfer
Insulated cook pots reduce heat transfer by over 90%, making them safer to handle and reducing the risk of burns. This is especially important in cold water conditions, where thermal regulation can be a safety concern.

### Material Integrity at Extreme Temperatures
Titanium's excellent mechanical properties ensure that even when exposed to extreme temperatures (both hot and cold), the pot will not suffer significant deformation or loss of strength, maintaining its integrity throughout the expedition.
