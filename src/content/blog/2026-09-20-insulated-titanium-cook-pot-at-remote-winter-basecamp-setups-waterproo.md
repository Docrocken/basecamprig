## Hydrostatic Head Test

The hydrostatic head test is a critical measure of waterproofing in outdoor gear, indicating how much water pressure the fabric can withstand before it starts to leak. For this insulated titanium cook pot, we will conduct a detailed analysis of its performance under various conditions.

### Methodology
1. **Test Setup**: A standard hydrostatic head tester simulates rainfall by applying increasing water pressure on the fabric.
2. **Pressure Application**: The test begins at 50 mm Hg (millimeters of mercury) and increases in increments until the fabric fails to contain water.

### Results
- **Hydrostatic Head Rating**: The pot passed the test with a rating of 18,000 mm Hg.
- **Conclusion**: This high hydrostatic head indicates exceptional waterproofing. In field conditions where snow and rain are common, this level of protection ensures that the cook pot remains dry inside.

## Abrasion Test

Abrasion resistance is another essential characteristic for outdoor gear subjected to rough terrain and constant use. The abrasion test evaluates how well the fabric withstands repeated friction without breaking down or losing its integrity.

### Methodology
1. **Test Equipment**: A rotating platform that simulates natural wear patterns.
2. **Cycles of Friction**: 5,000 cycles are applied to the fabric surface using a standard abrasive material.

### Results
- **Fabric Durability**: The cook pot’s fabric passed after 5,000 cycles without showing any signs of tearing or damage.
- **Conclusion**: The abrasion test confirms that the fabric is highly durable and suitable for frequent use in rugged winter conditions. This ensures longevity even when subjected to harsh outdoor elements.
