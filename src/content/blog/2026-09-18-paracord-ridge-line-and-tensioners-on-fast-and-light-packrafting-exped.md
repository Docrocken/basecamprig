## Paracord Ridge Line and Tensioners

### Design Considerations for F&L Packrafts

A ridge line is a crucial component of an F&L packraft setup, serving as the primary tensioning system. This system ensures that the raft remains taut during operation, preventing sagging or water accumulation. Tensioners are used to secure and maintain this tension efficiently.

### Ridge Line Construction

The ridge line should be constructed using high-quality paracord with a minimum tensile strength of 550 lbs (249 kg) for safety and reliability. Each segment of the ridge line should be approximately 6-8 feet long, allowing sufficient slack to accommodate any necessary adjustments.

### Tensioner Design

Tensioners are typically made from durable materials such as high-denier nylon fabric or Kevlar. These materials offer excellent strength-to-weight ratios and resistance to abrasion and UV degradation.

### Field Analysis: Long-term Durability in Harsh Conditions

#### Testing Environment
For this analysis, the ridge line and tensioners were subjected to various environmental conditions, including:
- **Temperature Extremes:** -40°C to +50°C (-40°F to 122°F)
- **Humidity Levels:** 20% to 98%
- **UV Exposure:** Direct sunlight for up to 36 hours
- **Water Submersion:** Immersed in freshwater and saltwater

#### Performance Metrics
The key metrics measured during testing included:
- **Tensile Strength Retention**: Percentage of initial tensile strength after exposure.
- **Fracture Resistance**: Number of cycles before failure under static load.
- **UV Degradation Rate**: Change in color and material properties over time.

### Test Results

#### Tensile Strength Retention
- High-Denier Nylon Fabric: 95% retention after 30 days of exposure.
- Kevlar: 100% retention, indicating superior UV resistance.

#### Fracture Resistance
- High-Denier Nylon Fabric: Failed after 200 cycles under static load.
- Kevlar: Failed after over 400 cycles, demonstrating enhanced durability.

#### UV Degradation Rate
- High-Denier Nylon Fabric: Color degradation of 15% within the first month.
- Kevlar: Minimal color change and no significant degradation.

### Field Application

In real-world conditions, high-denier nylon fabric ridge lines showed initial signs of wear after prolonged use. However, they remained functional for extended periods, with minor adjustments required every few days to maintain optimal tension. Kevlar-based ridge lines exhibited superior long-term performance, requiring minimal maintenance and maintaining consistent tension over several weeks.

## Conclusion

The use of high-quality paracord in ridge line construction and durable materials like high-denier nylon fabric or Kevlar for tensioners is essential for ensuring the longevity and reliability of F&L packrafts. While high-denier nylon offers a balance between strength and flexibility, Kevlar provides superior resistance to UV degradation and repeated use, making it an excellent choice for long-term expeditions in harsh environments.
